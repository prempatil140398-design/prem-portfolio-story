Decision 01

Multi-Channel AI Sales Automation

AI Automation / OyeLabs

Where a CRM becomes the risk, not the tool.

Problem

The CEO wanted all lead data stored directly inside GoHighLevel. That created long-term risks:

  • Sales users could accidentally delete leads
  • No centralized backup
  • Difficult salesperson management
  • Limited control over business data

Decision

Made Supabase the centralized database, with GoHighLevel acting only as the CRM layer. This gave permanent backup, better access control, easier salesperson management, and a scalable architecture for future automations.

Execution

Every lead enters Supabase first. GoHighLevel synchronizes from Supabase instead of acting as the source of truth. Salesperson availability is managed directly from Supabase, so adding or removing team members never breaks the workflow.

Outcome

Running in production for OyeLabs. The full sales pipeline operates with centralized data, improved security, and simplified administration.

Zero lead miss, down from ~20%

Decision 02

JennaHQ

FinTech / Canada / via OyeLabs

When pausing work protects the client.

Problem

The client provided only a dummy database. Starting AI development without validating production data could have meant rebuilding major components later.

Decision

Paused implementation and recommended validating the production database first, before any AI development began.

Execution

Prepared a discovery report, identified the dependency, and explained the delivery risks clearly to the client with evidence.

Outcome

The client accepted the recommendation. The project continued with zero blockers, confirming that addressing the risk early prevented delays later.

Decision 03

Travolyo

TravelTech / Saudi Arabia / via OyeLabs

A 2-month deadline with no margin.

Problem

A strict 2-month delivery timeline with almost no margin for delay. Waiting for the complete UI design before development would have created unnecessary bottlenecks.

Decision

Adopted an Agile delivery approach: development started as soon as the first approved screens were available, validating structure and functionality first and leaving visual refinements for later iterations.

Execution

Ran design and development in parallel, keeping continuous progress across multiple workstreams without blocking either team.

Outcome

Delivered the travel booking platform, including hotel, flight, villa, tour, and event modules, within the committed timeline.

Decision 04

Hunger Point

FoodTech / Lebanon / via OyeLabs

Zero meetings. Zero blockers. Full delivery.

Problem

The client preferred communicating only through WhatsApp and had limited technical knowledge. Traditional meetings and technical documentation would have slowed every decision.

Decision

Built the entire process around asynchronous collaboration: bilingual documentation (English and Arabic) plus recorded product walkthrough videos with Arabic explanations.

Execution

Every feature request, approval, credential request, and product update was documented in bilingual format. Communication stayed transparent without scheduled meetings.

Outcome

Delivered the complete project in 4 months without a single client meeting. Bilingual documentation improved client understanding and accelerated approvals.
